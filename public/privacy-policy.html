Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 | Last Updated: August 2026

At TokDownloader, reachable from our website, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document contains types of information that is collected and recorded by TokDownloader and how we use it.

1. Information We Collect

TokDownloader operates as a free web service. We do not require account registration, nor do we collect personal identification information such as your name, phone number, or physical address.

Note on Video Link Input: The TikTok video URLs submitted to our tool are used strictly to analyze, convert, and fetch media files. We do not store or track user video downloading histories on our servers.

2. Log Files

TokDownloader follows a standard procedure of using log files. These files log visitors when they visit websites. The information collected by log files includes:

  • Internet Protocol (IP) addresses
  • Browser type and version
  • Internet Service Provider (ISP)
  • Date and time stamp
  • Referring / exit pages

3. Cookies and Web Beacons

Like any other website, TokDownloader uses 'cookies'. These cookies are used to store information including visitors' preferences, and the pages on the website that the visitor accessed or visited. The information is used to optimize the users' experience by customizing our web page content based on visitors' browser type and/or other information.

4. Google DoubleClick DART Cookie & Third-Party Ads

Google is one of a third-party vendor on our site. It also uses cookies, known as DART cookies, to serve ads to our site visitors based upon their visit to our site and other sites on the internet.

Third-party ad servers or ad networks use technologies like cookies, JavaScript, or Web Beacons that are used in their respective advertisements and links that appear on TokDownloader. You may consult this list to find the Privacy Policy for each of the advertising partners of TokDownloader.
