TikTok Copyright & Fair Use Guidelines

Understanding intellectual property laws is critical when operating social media management tools and downloading third-party digital media.

What is Fair Use?

Fair use is a legal doctrine that promotes freedom of expression by permitting the unlicensed use of copyright-protected works in certain circumstances, such as commentary, criticism, news reporting, teaching, scholarship, and research.

Guidelines for Safe Content Use

  • Always Attribute: Give clear credit to the video author and tag their original profile.
  • Add Transformative Value: Do not simply repost identical clips. Add commentary, educational voiceovers, or original edits.
  • Avoid Monetizing Unlicensed Material: Commercial monetization of third-party audio or video without licensing rights can lead to legal penalties and account strikes.
